Sticky Sudoku Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

This policy explains how Sticky Sudoku (the "App") handles your data. The App is developed by Yu Te Lu ("we").

In short: we do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address or game content. Your game data stays on your device. The free version shows ads provided by Google, and Google collects some data to do so.

1. Data we do not collect

The App has no account system and we run no servers of our own. We do not collect, transmit or store your name, email address, phone number, contacts or precise location, and we do not receive your game progress or results. The App does not use any analytics tool of our own.

2. Data stored only on your device

The following is stored only on your device and is never sent to us:

  • Puzzles in progress, notes and saved snapshots
  • Completed games, times, daily challenge records and streaks
  • Your settings (language, hint and display options)
  • Whether you have purchased Sticky Sudoku Pure

Deleting the App deletes this data. If you use iCloud device backup, this data may be included in Apple's device backup, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

3. Device permissions the App uses

  • Camera: Used only when you choose to scan a puzzle QR code, to read the puzzle number. The image is processed on your device in real time. It is not stored and not sent anywhere.
  • Photos (add only): Used only when you tap "Save Image", to save your result picture to your photo library. The App does not read the photos in your library.
  • Clipboard: The App writes to the clipboard when you copy a puzzle number, and reads it when you paste on the puzzle-number screen, only to fill in the number.
  • Sharing: When you choose to share your result picture, it is handed to the app you pick, and that app's policy applies from there.

You can refuse any of these permissions and still play normally; only the related feature will be unavailable.

4. Ads and third-party services

The free version shows ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. The App includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's consent tool (User Messaging Platform).

To show ads and measure how they perform, Google may collect the following directly from your device. This data goes to Google; we do not receive data about individual users:

  • Device identifiers, such as the advertising identifier (IDFA)
  • IP address, and approximate location derived from it
  • Advertising data, such as the ads you have seen
  • Interactions with the App and with ads, such as app launches, ad taps and video ad views
  • Diagnostic and performance data, such as crash logs and launch time

Google uses this data to provide ads, for analytics, and to improve its services. See:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. Tracking and the advertising identifier

Before ads start, the App may show the iOS "Allow Tracking" prompt.

  • If you allow it, Google may use your device's advertising identifier to show personalised ads.
  • If you do not allow it, ads are still shown, but they are not personalised using the advertising identifier.

You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, certain US states or another region where the law requires it, the App first shows a consent form that lets you choose whether to allow data processing for advertising. You can reopen that form later in the App under Settings → About → Ad privacy settings to change or withdraw your choice.

6. In-app purchase

The App offers a one-time purchase, "Sticky Sudoku Pure", which removes ads. Payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never receive your card, payment method or Apple Account details; the App only learns from the App Store whether the Apple Account on this device owns the purchase.

After you buy Sticky Sudoku Pure, the App no longer starts the ads SDK and no longer shows the consent form or the tracking prompt, so the ad-related data collection described in sections 4 and 5 no longer takes place.

7. Children

The App is a puzzle game for a general audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your region). If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child's data has been collected through the App, please contact us using the details below.

8. Data retention and deletion

  • Game data on your device is kept until you delete the App. We hold no copy of it, so there is nothing we can delete or provide on your behalf.
  • Advertising data collected by Google is retained by Google under its own policies. You can limit collection as described in section 5, or exercise your rights with Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

9. Your rights

Depending on the law where you live (for example the EU GDPR or the California CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we hold no personal data about you ourselves, these rights are exercised mainly through the ad choices in section 5 and with Google. You are also welcome to contact us with any question.

We do not exchange your personal data for money. However, some US state laws treat making data available to advertising partners for personalised ads as "sharing" or "selling"; you can opt out as described in section 5.

10. Changes to this policy

If the way the App handles data changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Continuing to use the App means you accept the updated policy.
